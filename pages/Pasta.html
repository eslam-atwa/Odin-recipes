Description

A one-pot pasta dish where pasta, cherry tomatoes, spinach, garlic, and thinly sliced sausage simmer together in a fragrant broth until the noodles absorb the flavors—finished with a splash of cream and a generous sprinkle of Parmesan. Everything cooks in the same pan so the starch from the pasta thickens the sauce, creating a glossy, comforting coating without extra cleanup.

ingerdients:

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 6–8 oz sausage (or protein of choice)
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 4 cups spinach
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium; sauté sliced sausage 3–4 min until browned.
  2. Add garlic and tomatoes; cook 1–2 min until fragrant.
  3. Pour in broth, add pasta, bring to a boil then simmer, stirring, until pasta is nearly al dente (about 8–10 min).
  4. Stir in spinach, cream (if using), and Parmesan; cook 1–2 min until wilted and sauce thickens.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes; serve garnished with basil or extra Parmesan.
