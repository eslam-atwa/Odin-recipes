Scrambled Eggs

Homepage

Description

Fluffy scrambled eggs cooked low and slow for a creamy texture. Whisk eggs with a splash of milk, cook gently in butter, and stir occasionally until soft curds form. Perfect for breakfast with toast, hash browns, or as part of any meal.

ingerdients:

  • 6 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons milk or cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh chives or parsley (optional)

Steps:

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl and whisk together with milk, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in egg mixture and let sit for 20–30 seconds without stirring.
  4. Gently push eggs from the edges toward the center, stirring slowly and continuously.
  5. Cook 2–3 minutes until soft curds form and eggs are just set but still creamy.
  6. Remove from heat and serve immediately, garnished with chives or parsley if desired.
